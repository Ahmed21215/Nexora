Can I actually edit these, or is it a locked PDF?

Everything comes as an editable Google Doc or Word file, plus a Sheets/Excel version of every tracker. PDFs are only used for the short instruction guides.

How do I get the files after buying?

You'll get an instant download link by email after checkout, along with the "Start Here" guide so you know what to open first.

Do the templates work outside the US?

The outreach structure and tracking system apply broadly, though a few program-specific references assume a US application cycle.
