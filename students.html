Questions

Are the files editable?

Yes — every template is delivered as an editable Google Doc or Word file, with trackers provided in Sheets and Excel.

Which software do I need?

A free Google account (Docs and Sheets) or Microsoft Word and Excel. No special software is required.

Are these resources only for pre-med students?

No. The outreach and organization systems apply broadly to research and healthcare-adjacent paths, though a few references assume a US application timeline.

Do the products guarantee a position?

These tools are designed to help you organize your outreach and communicate more professionally — they don't guarantee an offer, interview, or acceptance.

Can I share the files?

Each purchase is licensed for individual use. Please don't redistribute the files to others.

Are future updates included?

Minor updates to a product you've purchased are included at no extra cost. Larger new products are released separately.

What is the refund policy?

Email support within 14 days of purchase and you'll get a full refund, no questions asked.

Can Nexora review my resume?

Yes — resume and outreach-email reviews are available separately from the personalized reviews section above.
